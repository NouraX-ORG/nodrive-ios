# Allowlist for tools/check-branding.py (spec section 6, "the no-Nextcloud rule").
#
# Format, one entry per line, fields separated by " | ":
#   <path glob> | <regex searched in the hit text, case-insensitive> | <class> | <reason>
# class: internal = an identifier the server, the OS or a library depends on (never shown)
#        legal    = a copyright or licence notice that must stay
#        artwork  = a generic image whose colour is close to a Nextcloud blue by chance; it was
#                   looked at and shows no Nextcloud logo or art. Applies to colour and pixel hits
#                   only; pin the exact colour or share, so a changed image is looked at again
# A hit is classified by the first entry whose glob matches its path and whose regex
# matches its text. Everything else fails the sweep. Keep regexes tight (anchor them):
# a user-visible text must never be covered by an entry.

# --- Swift module, Xcode internals --------------------------------------------------

iOSClient/NCBridgeSwift.h | ^Nextcloud-Swift\.h$ | internal | Objective-C header that Xcode generates for the Swift module "Nextcloud"
