# NoAgenda (iOS), step 1: core, read only (step 2, editing, is described below)

Binding spec: `plans/noagenda-decisions.md` (approved 2026-10-28). Platform plan: `plans/noagenda-ios-plan.md`
(section 2, "agenda-core"). Branch `feature/noagenda-core`. Code: `iOSClient/NoAgenda/`. Nothing in this step
writes to any server: no write request exists in the code (`NCAgendaMethod` has GET, PROPFIND and REPORT only).

## What the user gets

- **NoAgenda** is the first entry of the sidebar's **Apps** section, above the server's Workspace apps. The
  section is there even when the server lists no apps.
- **Day, Week and Month** lists of everything the person is responsible for: NoCalendar events, NoTasks to-dos,
  NoGantt tasks and milestones, NoBoard cards. Every row shows its kind, title, time or due date, the source in
  its colour, and the agent ("🤖 Writer-Bot" or "👤 Self").
- The chips **👤 Me, 🤖 My agents, 👥 My team** and the switch **All / Work / Personal**.
- **Day** sections: timed items, Due today, In progress, Late (today only), Problems with your agents, My team,
  and a folded "No date" list.
- **My team** (projects and boards the person runs): problems only (late, due today, blocked, someone overbooked,
  not assigned to a person, the integrity flags of design 4.4), and the switch **Show all team tasks**.
  Each sentence is one problem: a flag about the whole task shows once (not once per person), flags that read the
  same show once, and an agent of the person's own that is on a card or task without them is said once (as theirs).
  Problems count as Work, so they are hidden under **Personal**.
- **Sources**: every calendar, list, project and board with its setting (design 6.1), the working hours, and the
  agents of the registry. The first time, **"Which of your calendars are personal?"** is asked once.
- A row opens a detail sheet (read only here) with **Open in NoCalendar / NoTasks / NoGantt / NoBoard**, which
  opens the page signed in.
- Offline, the last data stays readable and is marked with the time it was saved. NoAgenda reads again when it
  opens, on pull to refresh and when the app comes back to the foreground while NoAgenda is shown. Background App
  Refresh updates it when iOS allows (it shares the app's one refresh task; no new background identifier). When iOS
  ends the background time, the refresh stops at its next check and the saved rows stay.
- The accent of the screens (chips, Today, Sources, the month selection) is the brand colour of the account's theme,
  like the other NoDrive screens.

## How it works

| Part | Files |
|---|---|
| One transport: only the account's own origin, no redirects, `OCS-APIRequest: true` on every request, the account's existing credential | `NCAgendaHTTP.swift` |
| CalDAV (events expanded by the server, to-dos without expand), NoGantt 2.4.1 (OCS routes), Deck REST | `NCAgendaCalendarReader.swift`, `NCAgendaICal.swift`, `NCAgendaDAVXML.swift`, `NCAgendaGanttReader.swift`, `NCAgendaDeckReader.swift` |
| The agent registry behind one swappable source (`NCAgendaRegistrySources.current`: the Drive file `/NoAgenda/agent-registry.json` now, the agents app later) | `NCAgendaRegistry.swift` |
| The rules (sources, agents, integrity, team, dedupe) as plain functions | `NCAgendaAssembler.swift`, `NCAgendaGanttAssembler.swift`, `NCAgendaSources.swift` |
| Day, Week, Month planning | `NCAgendaPlanner.swift` |
| Offline cache (JSON files in the app's own Application Support, one folder per account, excluded from backup, never a secret) and the store (one refresh at a time) | `NCAgendaCache.swift`, `NCAgendaStore.swift` |
| Screens | `NCAgendaRootView.swift`, `NCAgendaRowView.swift`, `NCAgendaDetailSheet.swift`, `NCAgendaSourcesView.swift`, `NCAgendaViewModel.swift` |

Hooks in upstream files are generated by `tools/rebrand.py` (`noagenda(st)`, ids A1 to A7): the sidebar item (A1 to
A3), opening it and keeping its selection (A4, A5), forgetting the account's cache on removal (A6), and the shared
background refresh (A7).

**Version guard** (design 5): Deck `1.19.x` may be written to (from the editing step); any other version is shown
read only. NoGantt has no readable version, so each answer is checked against the 2.4.1 contract in
`NCAgendaGanttContract`: a missing key (a nullable key may be null, but it must be there) or another type makes
NoGantt read only, and the rows that decoded are still shown. Rows of a home under the guard carry the lock. **Auth fallback**: a 401 or 403 from NoGantt or NoBoard shows a banner with **Open in NoGantt / NoBoard**;
the credential never goes anywhere else.

## Editing and conflicts (step 2, `feature/noagenda-edit`)

Binding spec: `plans/noagenda-decisions.md` sections 4.3 to 4.5, 5, 6.0, 6.1, 7 and 9. Platform plan: `plans/noagenda-ios-plan.md`
(section 3, "agenda-edit"). Code: `iOSClient/NoAgendaEdit/` (all new logic; strings in `en.lproj/NoAgendaEdit.strings`, translations are a
to-do). Step 1 files (`iOSClient/NoAgenda/`, NoDrive's own) got small hooks for the lock, the waiting mark and the clash mark; two are
commented as hooks of this step: `NCAgendaStore.isUnchanged` and `DeckBoard.canEdit`. No upstream Swift file is touched.

### What the user gets

- **Edit** (button in the detail sheet): title, start and end or due date, done, percent, description (up to 2,000 characters) and **who
  does it** (Self or one of my own agents) for an event, a to-do, a NoGantt task and a NoBoard card. **+** makes a new event or to-do in
  my own Personal or Work calendar or list (Self preselected). **Delete** of an event or to-do moves it to the calendar's trash bin.
- **Where it is written.** Events and to-dos by CalDAV (`PUT` with `If-Match` from a fresh read, a create with `If-None-Match: *`, so
  the rest of the item stays byte for byte: alarms, attendees, unknown properties, the UID). NoGantt tasks by `PUT tasks/{id}`
  (dates, percent, status, the planning person) with the follower tasks moving as NoGantt moves them, and **Undo** (`POST` of the
  operation id). NoBoard cards by the Deck API (due date, the agent label). Every write goes through `NCAgendaHTTP.makeRequest`: the
  account's own server, the existing app password, no redirects, no new credential.
- **Nothing is overwritten.** Each command is queued with the version of the item it started from. It reads the item fresh before the
  write; if the version moved, the other change stays and the command goes to **Not sent** with "Apply my change again", "Keep the other
  change" and "Open in ...". NoGantt and Deck have no conditional write, so a gap of milliseconds remains (server item below).
- **Offline.** A change without a connection waits (the row says "Waiting to be sent", the banner "Waiting to send n changes", with
  Review), survives a restart and goes out in order when the connection is back. Signed out holds everything until the person signs in.
- **Locks, with a reason and the signed-in "Open in ..." button:** repeating items, meetings someone else organises, viewer-only
  projects, read-only boards, items of a home that refuses NoDrive's sign-in or is not installed, and a row with a change waiting.
- **Clashes and workload (design 7).** Two timed Self items of mine that overlap are marked as a clash (a tentative one is a soft clash;
  agent items never clash with mine); the Conflicts chip lists only marked rows. The bar of the day and of the week shows planned hours
  (my own share of Gantt tasks, agent tasks included), free hours (my working time minus my busy Self events) and turns orange when
  overbooked. My own agents get their own "... is overbooked on ..." problem against their registry capacity, working days and days off.
- **Data safety.** No Gantt task or card is ever deleted. The only DELETE is the event or to-do delete; it checks again when it is sent
  and refuses a series, an object with several components and an invitation.

### Tests

Where each thing is tested is in `docs/testing.md` (rows "NoAgenda editing ..."). In short: unit tests (the writers, the patcher, the
queue, the rules; 27 NoAgenda suites), the **NoAgenda replay** job (the app's own request bytes against a real Nextcloud 35 with

### Choices recorded after the reviews

- **One Save, two commands.** A Gantt task or a card can make two commands from one Save (dates and agent, due date and agent). After the
  first is saved, the queue gives the second the new version of the item (NoGantt `updated_at` or Deck `lastModified`, from the answer of
  the save, or read when the answer does not say it). Only that version is passed on; a real change by someone else before the first
  save is still a conflict for both.
- **Date-only to-dos.** The patcher keeps the value type of `DTSTART` and `DUE` (a date stays `VALUE=DATE`) and writes a new one in the
  type of the other. A start after the due date, and an end before the start, are refused in the editor.
- **Statuses.** The status picker offers `not_started`, `in_progress` and `blocked`; done is the Done switch. This is the list NoGantt 2.4.1
  accepts (`StatusValidator`, as read for desktop and Android). The replay job sends a status change to the real server.
- **Agent on a Gantt task.** The planning person of an agent is the one already on the task, then one on another task of the project, then
  one in the pool of the project's owner (`owner_id` of the resource). A same-named person of another manager's pool is never taken. The
  agent's own load counts a name-only planning person only next to my linked planning person on the same task, as the core does.
- **A home that takes no change.** Rows of NoGantt or NoBoard that is refused, read only or not installed are locked with the reason (the
  sheet has "Open in ..."). What waited for it goes to Not sent with that reason. Only an offline or unreachable home holds changes.
  The banner "Waiting to send ..." has a Review button that lists what waits.
- **A waiting row is locked** ("This change is waiting to be sent."), so the folding of a second change into a waiting one (plan 3.5) cannot
  happen from the screen. This is on purpose: it also rules out a change during a send. The queue still folds if two commands are added.
- **Free time** subtracts the person's own busy events (design 7); timed cards are not subtracted. This follows the design; plan 3.6 also
  names timed cards. A one-day Gantt task at NoGantt's working hours counts as timed and clashes with every Self event of that day. This is
  noisy and is a question for all three apps. It is left as the design words it ("Gantt tasks and cards with set times"), the same on Android,
  so the apps do not drift apart; a change needs one decision for all three.
- **The toast** view lives in `NCAgendaWorkloadBar.swift` and its model in `NCAgendaViewModel+Edit.swift`; the plan names `NCAgendaToast.swift`.
  It stays where it is, not because of the project file: `APP_SOURCE_FOLDERS` of `tools/rebrand.py` already adds every `.swift` file of
  `iOSClient/NoAgendaEdit/`, so a separate file would cost nothing.
- **A description that was not read is not editable (review 3).** An event or to-do cached by the core build knows the length of its
  description but not the text. The editor then says "not loaded yet, Open in NoCalendar" like for a description over 2,000 characters, and a
  draft never changes it. The store also reads such a calendar once more at the next refresh (`NCAgendaStore.isUnchanged` is false while a
  cached description of 2,000 characters or less has no text), so the editor gets the text without waiting for the 1st of the month.
- **Delete checks again when it is sent.** Like the change, a queued delete reads the object fresh and refuses a series, an object with
  several components and an invitation (organised by someone else).
- **Signed out holds the commands.** While the account is signed out (a read answered 401 or 403) no command is sent, and a CalDAV 401 on the
  way is held like a lost connection, not moved to Not sent. They go out after the person signs in again.
- **Cards on a board without edit rights are locked** up front (`PERMISSION_EDIT` of the board, read with the boards). A board that did not
  say (cached before) is not locked; NoBoard's answer still ends in Not sent.
- **Invitations**: the lock text says the organiser changes the meeting; the agent stays Self.
- **A failed refresh after a save** shows the old cached row until the next refresh (the saved values are not kept drawn). Open nit; the
  replay now also has an all-day event scenario (`event-all-day`, 17 scenarios).
- **Web edit proof (design 4.2).** `tools/ci/agenda-webedit.py` drives the Calendar and Tasks pages as the desktop job does and now fails
  when neither page could be edited (a page that was not driven is a warning in the job summary). The proof that the id and the tag survive
  a web edit is the desktop job (Calendar: both survive; Tasks: the id is dropped, the tag survives) and the Android job (both survive in
  both pages). The row for the owner stays open.

## Timeline (step 3, `feature/noagenda-timeline`)

Binding spec: `plans/noagenda-decisions.md` section 8 (Timeline) and 9. Platform plan: `plans/noagenda-ios-plan.md` section 4. Code: `iOSClient/NoAgendaTimeline/`
(all new logic; strings in `en.lproj/NoAgendaTimeline.strings`, translations are a to-do). No upstream file is touched and `tools/rebrand.py` gets no hook,
only the new folder and the two new test files. Small marked changes in NoDrive's own files of steps 1 and 2: the view picker has **Timeline**
(`AgendaViewKind`), the editor sheet has "Move by", a dependency remembers the person on its predecessor, and the Team chip is dimmed on the Timeline.

### What the user gets

- **A fourth view, Timeline**, next to Day, Week and Month, with the chips Me and My agents, the All/Work/Personal switch and the Conflicts chip. My team is dimmed:
  team work is not drawn here. The range is seven days before the shown date to eight weeks after it; the arrows move it by four weeks, Today scrolls back. Zoom
  **Weeks** (12 points a day) or **Days** (40); the name column (120 points on a phone) and the scale (months, weeks, days) stay in place while the rest scrolls.
- **Bars** for my NoGantt tasks, grouped under the project and the phase (items without a phase first, by start; then the phases by their first start), with the
  **% done filling the bar**. My to-dos with a start and a due date (not repeating) are thin dashed bars under "NoTasks > list". **Diamonds** for the milestones of
  the projects where I have tasks and for my dated cards ("NoBoard > board"). **The agent** ("🤖 Writer-Bot", "👤 Self") and the marks (late, blocked, locked,
  clash, day off, waiting, not sent) follow every bar; a milestone has no agent (NoGantt milestones have no assignee). Phases are grouped by the phase id, so two
  phases of one project with the same name stay two groups.
- **Dependency arrows**, read only, for finish-to-start, start-to-start, finish-to-finish and start-to-finish (NoGantt 2.4.1 answers them as `EA`, `AA`, `EE` and `AE`;
  `FS`, `SS`, `FF`, `SF` are read the same). A predecessor that is not on the screen is a short **stub**; tapping it says "After: Publish banners (Sara)".
- **My busy time** shades the day behind the bars (0 to 35 % of the accent colour, by the share of my working hours my own busy events take; an agent's event and a
  free event never shade). Days off are hatched (not a working day, a holiday, an all-day busy event). A line marks today.
- **Tap** a bar or diamond for the detail sheet. Its editor has the two date pickers and **Move by** -7, -1, +1, +7 days. There is **no drag on phones**. A move is the
  same command as setting the two dates by hand: a NoGantt `PUT` with `start_date`, `end_date` and `adjust_lag: true` (the followers move; Undo in the toast) or, for a to-do,
  a CalDAV change that keeps the times of day. Queue, conflicts and locks are those of step 2. A task without a start date is a one-day bar with a lock ("This task has no
  dates to move."); the date pickers of such a task are off (a task with a start and no end can be moved: the editor has the start). A locked bar (viewer project, repeating, waiting change, refused home) has no Edit button, so no date editor.
- **ⓘ** opens the legend. Every bar and diamond has a spoken label ("Gantt task, Write homepage copy, 27 Oct to 30 Oct, 40 % done, Writer-Bot"), and the arrows are read
  as "Review banners comes after Write homepage copy".

### Choices

- **No Swift Charts.** Arrows and a tap target for every bar are simpler with views and `Canvas` (plan 4.6).
- **Own shards.** The Timeline runs in two shards of its own (`agenda-timeline`, `agenda-timeline-dark`) beside the editing shards, so a flake in one does not hide the other and the
  wall time does not grow; the plan had put it into the `agenda` shard.
- **Name column and scale.** A horizontal scroll view holds the scale above a vertical one; the name cells follow the scroll offset (`onScrollGeometryChange` from iOS 18,
  a preference before that), so a name never scrolls away.
- **Left to right in every language.** The chart is forced to a left-to-right layout (also in Arabic): the bars are placed by offset and the scale, shading and
  arrows are drawn on canvases, and the two must not mirror differently. The controls above the chart and the sheets follow the language; the names inside the chart are left-aligned (RTL is not yet checked by a screenshot).
- **Banners.** On the Timeline the banners of the data take at most 140 points and scroll past that, so a small phone keeps its chart. The empty text ("No dated work in
  this range.") shows only after the first read. The last row can be scrolled 96 points clear of the floating tab bar.
